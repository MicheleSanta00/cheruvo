How it works

From raw news to actionable signal

Type any US or European symbol. Cheruvo validates it and loads price, sector and exchange data instantly.

FinBERT reads every headline and assigns a score from −1 (very bearish) to +1 (very bullish). No keyword tricks — real language understanding.
